# AP English Language and Composition Course Skills

## Big Idea 1: Rhetorical Situation (RHS)
* **Skill 1.A (Reading)**: Identify and describe components of the rhetorical situation (exigence, purpose, audience, writer, and context).
* **Skill 2.A (Writing)**: Make strategic choices in a text that address a specific rhetorical situation.

## Big Idea 2: Claims and Evidence (CLE)
* **Skill 3.A (Reading)**: Identify and explain claims and evidence within an argument.
* **Skill 4.A (Writing)**: Develop a paragraph that includes a claim and evidence supporting that claim.
* **Skill 3.B (Reading)**: Identify and describe the overarching thesis of an argument, including structural indicators.
* **Skill 4.B (Writing)**: Write a thesis statement that requires proof or defense and previews the argument structure.
* **Skill 3.C (Reading)**: Explain ways claims are qualified through modifiers, counterarguments, or alternative perspectives.
* **Skill 4.C (Writing)**: Qualify a claim using modifiers, counterarguments, or alternative perspectives.

## Big Idea 3: Reasoning and Organization (REO)
* **Skill 5.A (Reading)**: Describe the line of reasoning and the relationships between its parts.
* **Skill 6.A (Writing)**: Develop a line of reasoning and create structural relationships between its parts.
* **Skill 5.B (Reading)**: Explain how the organization of a text creates unity and coherence.
* **Skill 6.B (Writing)**: Use transitional elements to guide readers through a line of reasoning and ensure coherence.
* **Skill 5.C (Reading)**: Recognize and describe how a writer's word choice reveals biases, logical fallacies, or underlying assumptions.
* **Skill 6.C (Writing)**: Refine writing to address personal biases or intentional rhetorical strategies.

## Big Idea 4: Style (STL)
* **Skill 7.A (Reading)**: Explain how stylistic choices like diction, syntax, and imagery contribute to tone.
* **Skill 8.A (Writing)**: Strategically select words, syntax, and tone to convey a deliberate style.
* **Skill 7.B (Reading)**: Analyze how a writer's choices in punctuation and mechanics serve a rhetorical purpose.
* **Skill 8.B (Writing)**: Apply grammar, punctuation, and mechanics intentionally to enhance an argument.
* **Skill 7.C (Reading)**: Evaluate how a text's style shifts across different sections or modes of development.
* **Skill 8.C (Writing)**: Maintain stylistic choices across complex or extended lines of reasoning.
