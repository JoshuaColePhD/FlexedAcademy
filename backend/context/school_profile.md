# School Profile: Florence High School

## Logistics
- **Class Length**: 50 minutes per period. Do not plan 90-minute block activities.
- **Schedule Type**: 5 days a week (Monday-Friday).

## 2026-2027 Calendar Exceptions (No School / Special Days)
When generating a weekly plan, if any of these dates fall within the requested week, mark that specific day as `no_school: true` and do not plan instruction for it.

- **August 5, 2026**: First Day for Students
- **September 7, 2026**: Labor Day (No School)
- **October 8-9, 2026**: Parent/Teacher Conferences / Edcamp (No School for Students)
- **October 12-16, 2026**: Fall Break (No School)
- **November 11, 2026**: Veteran’s Day (No School)
- **November 23-27, 2026**: Thanksgiving Break (No School)
- **December 17, 2026**: Early Dismissal (End of Semester 1)
- **December 18, 2026 - January 6, 2027**: Winter Break (No School)
- **January 7, 2027**: Students Return (Start of Semester 2)
- **January 18, 2027**: MLK Jr. Holiday (No School)
- **February 15, 2027**: President’s Day (No School)
- **March 12, 2027**: Data Day (No School for Students)
- **March 22-26, 2027**: Spring Break (No School)
- **April 30, 2027**: FLCN Pacing (No School for Students)
- **May 27, 2027**: Last Day for Students
